The source

A real retailer, with the edges left visible

CC BY 4.0

Online Retail II · Daqing Chen, UCI Machine Learning Repository.

1,067,371 original line items from a UK non-store retailer. Many customers are wholesalers. Original coverage: 1 December 2009 to 9 December 2011. Meridian is a fictional brand used for this demonstration.

Open the original dataset ↗

What these numbers mean

  • Net merchandise sales = positive merchandise sales minus credit transactions. This is not audited accounting revenue or profit.
  • Administrative lines and non-positive unit prices are excluded. Exact duplicate rows are removed as an explicit demo policy.
  • December 2011 is excluded because it is incomplete. Anonymous transactions remain in sales totals but not customer analyses.
  • Segmentation uses stated rules. Historical patterns and hypothetical uplifts do not prove business outcomes.
